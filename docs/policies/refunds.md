### Refunds & Duplicate Charges
Customers may request a refund within 30 days of the charge. Refunds are issued to the original payment method and appear within 5-10 business days.

Duplicate charges are always refunded in full, no questions asked. If a customer reports being charged twice for the same period, verify both invoices exist for the same billing period, then refund the later charge.

Refunds over $50 require approval from a billing specialist before being issued. Support agents may issue refunds of $50 or less directly when the request clearly matches policy.

A customer may receive at most 2 good will refunds per calendar year. Requests beyond that limit must be escalated to a human specialist.
