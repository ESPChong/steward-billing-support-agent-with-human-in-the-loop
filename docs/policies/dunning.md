### Dunning & Failed Payments
When a payment fails we retry the card 3 times over 10 days. The customer is emailed after each attempt with a link to update their payment method.

Accounts remain fully functional during the first two retries. After the third failure the subscription moves to past_due and features are suspended after 14 days.

If the customer confirms they already paid, verify invoices and payment status before responding. Never threaten account cancellation while a payment dispute is open — escalate to a human specialist instead.
